Featured Product

ReceiptsDock

Automated WhatsApp receipt and expense tracking built for UK tradespeople.

WhatsApp Integrated

Turn WhatsApp into an automated van bookkeeper.

ReceiptsDock allows plumbers, electricians, builders, and contractors to snap photos of merchant receipts (Screwfix, Toolstation, Travis Perkins) and forward them via WhatsApp. Our vision models automatically extract totals, line items, and VAT for seamless tax filing.

  • ✓ Zero app download required — runs entirely within WhatsApp
  • ✓ Real-time optical character recognition (OCR) and VAT extraction
  • ✓ Formatted export ready for self-assessment tax returns
